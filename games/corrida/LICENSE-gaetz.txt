Original project: https://github.com/Gaetz/js-Racing
Adapted for JogaZone as "Corrida a Dois": car physics, track-grid collision
and win-detection logic are ported from the original (translated variable
comments, WASD instead of ZQSD for player 1). Tile/car graphics were
redrawn from scratch as flat canvas shapes in the JogaZone colour palette
instead of using the original PNG sprites.
